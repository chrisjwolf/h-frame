As you would expect, some of the most common questions about vintage Workmates concern replacement parts and repairs. No original parts are available through Black & Decker for the Workmate 79-001 or other models from the 1970's or early 1980's for the U.S. and the UK. Perhaps one day someone will discover a treasure trove of new-old-stock parts somewhere. If you hear of one, please let me know!

The links on the left cover some of the most often requested parts.

You'll see four main strategies I suggest if you need parts:
  • Search on eBay. Quite a few original parts are available from sellers who have scavenged them from vintage Workmates.
  • Search for equivalent modern parts. For example, see my recommendations for replacement feet and vise handles.
  • Make your own replacements. For example, see my page about making a replacement top, or my page about plastic lever repair.
  • Look for 3D printed parts, or model files to print them. There are many of these available for vintage Workmates.

I'm sure there are owners out there who have more to contribute on this topic, so I want to make this a clearinghouse for such information. If you have parts, products, services, or procedures to recommend for keeping older Workmates looking good and working well, please contact me.
