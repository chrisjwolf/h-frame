Workmate 79-001 Accessories

Workmate Horizontal Clamp 79-018

The 79-018 Horizontal Clamp was the last Workmate accessory to be introduced, first appearing in their catalog in 1983. It was also the first one made in Taiwan rather than Canada. It sold for $6.49.

The catalog says: "Increases clamping capacity and provides extra stability and pressure for large objects. Can also work in workbenches by drilling a 13/16" hole."
