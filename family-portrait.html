Workmate 79-001 Family Portrait

Here they are, together for the first time ever, ten different Workmate 79-001's from the earliest Type to the latest.

Front center is the Type E, flanked to the left by a Type 1 and to the right by a Type 2. The second row has a Type 3 on the left, followed by a Type 4 with aluminum H-frame, another Type 4 with steel H-frame, and a Type 6. The third row has Types 7, 8, and 9.
